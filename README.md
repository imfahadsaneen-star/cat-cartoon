# Cat Cartoon

An emotional 3D animated story about two adorable kitten siblings.

## Story

A brother and sister have a small fight. The angry sister runs into a dangerous jungle, where a huge bear threatens her. Her brave brother rescues her.

She cries and apologizes. He tells her:

> "Don't cry, little sister. I will always protect you."
